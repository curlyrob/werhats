What the app does

  • Log what you’ve lent or borrowed – with person, date and notes.
  • Take or choose photos of items.
  • Set a return date and get reminded via the Reminders app and/or your calendar.
  • Pick the person from your contacts and send a friendly, ready-made reminder by message, email or any other app.

FAQ

I don’t get a reminder.

The app adds reminders to the Reminders app and events to your calendar. Check in Settings › Privacy & Security › Reminders or › Calendars that the app has access, and that notifications are allowed for Reminders and Calendar.

Where are my entries stored?

Only on your iPhone. There’s no account and no server. Your entries are included in your regular iPhone backup (iCloud or computer).

How do I mark something as returned?

Open the entry and tap “I Got It Back” or “I Gave It Back” – or swipe right in the list. The reminder is then checked off and the calendar event removed.

Why doesn’t the app ask for access to all my contacts?

It uses the iOS contact picker, so it only receives the one contact you tap – never your whole address book.

How do I delete all data?

Delete single entries by swiping left. Deleting the app removes all entries and photos. Reminders and calendar events already created can be deleted in those apps if needed.
