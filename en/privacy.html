Privacy Policy

for the iPhone app “Who’s Got It?” (German: “Wer hat’s?”)

Support Privacy Deutsch

In short

The app does not collect any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone. There is no account, no server, no advertising, no tracking and no analytics. As the developer, I receive no data from the app.

Controller

Robert Richter
Email: curlyrob@gmail.com

Data stored on your device

To work, the app stores locally on your iPhone:

  • the items you enter (name, notes, dates, return date),
  • the person’s name and – if you pick them from your contacts – their phone number, email address and contact photo,
  • photos you take or select for an item.

This data is not transferred to me or any third party. It is part of your regular iPhone backup, managed by Apple or your computer according to your own settings.

Permissions

Camera

Only when you take a photo. The photo is stored only within the app.

Photos

Selection uses the iOS photo picker. The app only sees the pictures you select and has no access to the rest of your library.

Contacts

Selection uses the iOS contact picker. The app only receives the one contact you tap and has no access to the rest of your address book.

Calendars and Reminders

Only if you turn on a reminder or calendar event for an item. The app then creates, updates and removes entries – and only reads the entries it created itself. Whether these entries sync to other devices (e.g. via iCloud) depends on your iOS settings.

You can change all permissions at any time in your iPhone’s Settings › Privacy & Security.

Sending messages

When you send someone a reminder, the app opens Messages, Mail or the iOS share sheet with a suggested text. Nothing is sent until you tap “Send” yourself – via the app or service you choose. That service’s privacy policy applies to the transmission.

Deletion

You can delete entries in the app at any time. Deleting the app removes all data stored in it.

Your rights

As I do not process any data about you, there is no data I could provide information about, correct or delete. You are still welcome to contact me by email with any privacy questions. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

Changes

If data processing changes – for example due to new features – this policy will be updated.
